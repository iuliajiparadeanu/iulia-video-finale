# Prompt for DJ images with a faithful face (ChatGPT / Gemini)

## What to upload with the prompt (all together, in one message)
1. 2 sharp photos of his face from the front: the photos in front of the beige wall (crossed arms).
2. The real photo on the boat at the decks (shows how he stands at the decks).
3. The frame from the video you want as the scene (e.g. `01_la-platane_mixaj-apus.jpg`).

Tip: Gemini ("Nano Banana") usually keeps faces closer than ChatGPT. If the face still drifts, generate again from the same message instead of asking "fix the face" in the same chat.

## Prompt (in English, it works better)

```
Use the uploaded portrait photos as the ONLY identity reference. The man in the output must be
exactly this real person, not a lookalike. Preserve his face 1:1:
- full, rounded face with wide cheeks and a soft jawline (not angular, not slimmed)
- broad nose with a rounded, fleshy tip
- small, slightly deep-set eyes, light grey-blue
- short grey-blond stubble beard, thin upper lip
- shoulder-length blond-grey hair, slicked straight back
- tanned skin, natural age ~55, natural wrinkles, no beautifying, no de-aging, no face slimming

Scene: editorial event-poster photograph of him DJing at sunset on a rooftop terrace high above a
city, with Mount Vesuvius on the horizon. He stands behind a Pioneer DJ setup (two CDJs and a
mixer), both hands on the mixer, relaxed and confident, calm expression, slight smile, looking
toward the camera. Wearing gold-frame aviator sunglasses with brown lenses, black blazer over an
open black shirt, silver necklaces, black headphones with teal ear cushions around his neck,
watch and leather bracelets.

Photography: shot on 35mm, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, warm golden-hour light, soft
pastel sky, subtle film grain, high detail, sharp focus on face and hands, vertical 2:3.
Do not change his facial features, face shape, nose or hairline.
```

## Variant without sunglasses (if you want his eyes visible)
Replace the line about sunglasses with:
`Sunglasses pushed up on top of his head, eyes visible, gentle smile.`
